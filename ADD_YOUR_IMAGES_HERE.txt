📸 HOW TO ADD YOUR IMAGES TO THE PORTFOLIO
==========================================

STEP 1: Copy your 6 images to the "images" folder

STEP 2: Rename them as follows:
   - profile.jpg  → Your main profile photo (the one you want in the hero section)
   - img1.jpg     → Night photo with yellow hoodie
   - img2.jpg     → Train photo
   - img3.jpg     → Building entrance photo
   - img4.jpg     → Traditional attire photo
   - img5.jpg     → Corridor photo (optional - not currently used)
   - img6.jpg     → Night selfie (optional - not currently used)

STEP 3: Supported formats
   ✓ .jpg
   ✓ .jpeg
   ✓ .png
   ✓ .webp

STEP 4: Recommended image sizes
   - profile.jpg: 400x400px (square)
   - img1-4: 300x300px or larger

NOTE: If images are not found, placeholder images will be shown automatically.

After adding images, refresh your browser to see them!
