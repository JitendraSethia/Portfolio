📄 HOW TO ADD YOUR RESUME/CV TO THE PORTFOLIO
==============================================

STEP 1: Prepare Your Resume
   - Save your resume as a PDF file
   - Make sure it's professional and up-to-date
   - Keep file size under 2MB for fast downloads

STEP 2: Rename Your Resume
   - Rename the file to: Jitendra_Sethia_Resume.pdf
   - Exact name is important!

STEP 3: Add to Portfolio
   - Copy your resume file
   - Paste it into the "assets" folder in your portfolio directory
   - Path should be: PORTFOLIO/assets/Jitendra_Sethia_Resume.pdf

STEP 4: Test It
   - Refresh your portfolio website
   - Click the "Download Resume" button in the Contact section
   - Your resume should download automatically!

✅ DONE! Your resume is now available for download on your portfolio.

NOTE: If you want to use a different filename, update line in index.html:
   href="assets/YOUR_FILENAME.pdf"
